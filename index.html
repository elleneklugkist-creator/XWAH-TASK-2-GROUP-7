FAQ

Good to know

How much do the packages and experiences cost?

Every gaming package is R1,500 and every individual experience is R750. 15% VAT is added to every total.

Do you offer a discount if I book more than one thing?

Yes. Book 2 items and get 5% off the total, 3 items for 10% off and 4 or more for 15% off. The discount is taken off first and the VAT is added after.

How do I book, and when do I find out the final price?

Go to Calculate Fees, tick what you want and fill in your contact details, event type, date and address. Your quoted total shows straight away. A consultant then contacts you to confirm the booking and the total. The quote is not a formal invoice.

Do I have to travel to a venue?

Not at all. LvL UP is based at Emeris Pretoria in Lynnwood, and we bring the arena to you. You choose the event address and the date when you book.

What is included in the Ultimate Gamer Pass?

A full day of unlimited gaming with PC gaming, console gaming, high-speed internet, a snack voucher and tournament entry.

Can you host birthdays, school outings or corporate events?

Yes. The Birthday Party Package includes a reserved area, multiplayer competitions, decorations, catering and tournament prizes. We also host school outings and corporate team-building, and the Escape Room and Racing Simulator challenges work well for teams.

Choose your packages and experiences and we will work out your quote, with your discount and VAT included.

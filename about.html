About Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena

Established 2023 · Johannesburg · Founded by Jason Naidoo

[Image: founder Jason Naidoo and the arena]

History: Our Origins

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who wanted to create a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages could enjoy the latest gaming technology, compete in esports tournaments and socialise with friends. He noticed that many gamers lacked access to high-performance gaming equipment and organised competitive events, so he established an arena that caters for casual players, competitive gamers, schools and corporate events.

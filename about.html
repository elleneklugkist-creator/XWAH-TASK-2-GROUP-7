About us

About LvL UP

Established 2023 · Pretoria · Founded by Jason Naidoo

LvL UP was founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who wanted to create a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages could enjoy the latest gaming technology, compete in esports tournaments and socialise with friends. He noticed that many gamers lacked access to high-performance gaming equipment and organised competitive events, so he established an arena that caters for casual players, competitive gamers, schools and corporate events.
